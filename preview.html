About Abbotsford Table Society

A Shared Dream for Abbotsford

Abbotsford Table Society exists to unite churches and believers across our city to meet real needs in practical, relational, and collaborative ways — so that our community experiences the love of Christ through the people of God.

In June 2025, we launched the Abbotsford Table Society to bring this vision to life — fostering holistic care and relational wholeness through connected partnerships. Together, we’re uniting our community in hope and transformation.

"For years, God has been stirring in us a vision — one that goes beyond any single church or agency. It’s a dream for a city where the vulnerable are seen, where gaps in care are understood, and where hope and healing flow through the love of Jesus."

Objectives:

Uniting Our Community in Hope and Action

The Abbotsford Table Society exists to bring our community together — churches, non-profits, government agencies, and local leaders — to support our most vulnerable citizens through strategic partnerships and collaborative solutions. We believe lasting change happens when we connect, collaborate, and catalyze.

Strategic Framework

Our Three Pillars

Connection

We’re building a network of trusted relationships that bridges sectors and strengthens unity:

  • Building Trusted Partnerships
    Fostering strong, mission-aligned relationships across churches, agencies, and community leaders.
  • Mapping Community Assets
    Identifying services, strengths, and gaps within partner organizations to better understand the landscape.
  • Assessing Needs and Gaps
    Conducting regular assessments to inspire meaningful, data-informed responses.
  • Bridging Across Sectors
    Creating safe, neutral spaces for diverse groups to connect, align, and act together.

Collaboration

Working together, we tackle community needs with shared purpose. Key strategies include:

  • Launching Care Portal
    Leading the rollout of CarePortal—an online platform that connects real-time needs with local responders.
  • Mobilizing Local Responders
    Activating churches and volunteers to meet urgent, vetted needs in our community.
  • Hosting Collaborative Gatherings
    Organizing roundtables, workshops, and initiatives that align vision and action across sectors.
  • Tracking Progress and Impact
    Measuring engagement, evaluating outcomes, and reporting back to ensure accountability and learning.

Catalyzing Change

Supporting long-term solutions through innovation and leadership:

  • Encouraging new, community-led ministries
  • Using an asset-based approach
  • Piloting & scaling what works
  • Developing local leaders for long-term transformation

For Partner Agencies

For Local Church Care Teams

Churches across North, South, East, and West Abbotsford enroll their care teams to receive local request alerts and serve families in their immediate neighborhood.

  • Zero-cost implementation for local congregations
  • Geographic neighborhood matching in Abbotsford
